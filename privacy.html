Privacy Policy

Dijkstra · Effective 28 September 2026

Dijkstra is a personal, non-commercial AI assistant built and operated by Liam Cheung ("I", "me"). It is used only by me, on Google accounts that I own or am authorised to use. This policy explains what Google user data Dijkstra accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data accessed

With your explicit consent through Google's OAuth screen, Dijkstra may access:

  • Gmail (gmail.modify, gmail.labels): reading messages, creating drafts, sending email on request, and applying or managing labels. Dijkstra does not permanently delete email.
  • Google Calendar (calendar): reading calendars and events, and creating, updating or removing events on request.

2. How the data is used

Google user data is used only to provide Dijkstra's user-facing features for the account owner: triaging and labelling email, drafting and sending replies, and managing calendar events. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to these features.

3. AI processing

To carry out a requested task, relevant email or calendar content may be sent to Anthropic's Claude models for processing. Dijkstra itself does not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalised AI or machine-learning models. Content processed by Claude is subject to Anthropic's privacy policy.

4. Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own device and are never published or shared. Dijkstra processes email and calendar content to complete a task and does not sell it or build a separate archive of it on third-party servers.

5. Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, except (a) with Anthropic as a processor, as described in section 3, (b) where required by law, or (c) where the account owner explicitly instructs Dijkstra to send it (for example, sending an email).

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Dijkstra's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After revocation, Dijkstra can no longer access the account and its stored tokens stop working. For any other deletion request, contact me using the address below.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
