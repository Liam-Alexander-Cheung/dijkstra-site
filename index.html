Dijkstra

A personal AI assistant by Liam Cheung

Dijkstra is a private, non-commercial project. It is an AI orchestrator that helps its developer manage his own email and calendar: sorting and labelling messages, drafting and sending replies he asks for, and creating or updating calendar events.

Who can use it

Dijkstra is not a public service. It is used only by its developer, on his own Google accounts. There is no sign-up, and nothing is offered to the public.

Google data

Dijkstra connects to Gmail and Google Calendar through Google's OAuth, and only for accounts that have explicitly granted access. How that data is handled is described in the privacy policy.
